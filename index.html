Range rules

Safety comes before scores

Every visitor hears these rules before handling any equipment. The club's own written rules replace this sample list.

  1. Treat every firearm as loadedCheck the chamber and action whenever you pick one up.
  2. Muzzle always points downrangeIncluding when loading, unloading and handing over.
  3. Finger off the trigger until on targetRest it along the frame until you are ready to fire.
  4. Follow the range officer's commandsFire only on command and stop at once on a cease-fire call.
